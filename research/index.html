research

My work, grouped by the methods behind it.

Machine learning

Models that predict how a polymer or a liquid behaves from its chemical structure, built for the small datasets that are common in polymer science.

Graph neural networks for surface tension

Why surface tension matters, why it is hard to measure, and how a graph neural network that also learns from liquids predicts it for polymers. Try it in the surface tension predictor.

Digital Chemical Engineering 2024

A neural network in place of a process simulator

A deep neural network trained on a million process-simulator runs stands in for the simulator, so a natural-gas liquefaction cycle can be optimized in minutes instead of a day.

Molecular dynamics

Atom-by-atom simulations of polymer chains, films, and solvents, often run side by side with experiments.

Third Frontier outreach

Polymer outreach

Showing students how polymer chains stretch, tangle, and snap, with simulations and hands-on experiments.

Nature Communications 2026

Polymer chains built to respond to force

Molecular dynamics of three mechanophore-bearing polymers shows how stiff or flexible backbones shape the chains, alongside experiments on how the polymers respond to force.

Soft Matter 2024

Solvent moving through polystyrene films

Analysis of all-atom simulations, each longer than half a microsecond, of how a solvent enters glassy and molten polystyrene films.

Quantum chemistry

DFT and multireference methods for chemistry in which bonds break and form, including reactions driven by mechanical force.

Multireference ab initio dynamics

Pulling a molecular ring open

Quantum-chemistry simulations of a strained carbon ring torn open by force, and of what it does when the force lets go.

Physical Chemistry Chemical Physics 2023

Separating nitrogen from natural gas in a zeolite

A DFT and molecular dynamics study, with measured isotherms, of why nitrogen and methane move so differently through the pores of a sodium zeolite.
